Looply is finally on the App Store

A small app. A real release. Quite a lot learned along the way.

Today, 16 September 2026, I officially released Looply: Predictive reminders on the App Store.

The idea is still simple: Remember what repeats. Log when something happens, let Looply learn the rhythm, and get a reminder when it is likely to be needed again.

What started as a practical problem at home has made it through prototypes, TestFlight, and the less glamorous details of getting an app into the store. Seeing it available to download feels rather different from seeing it run on my own phone.

Now comes the next part: learning from people using it in everyday life.
